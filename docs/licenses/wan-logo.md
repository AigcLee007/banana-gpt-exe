# Wan brand Logo

`src/assets/wan-logo.ico` is the original favicon served by the official [Wan website](https://wan.video/), downloaded on 2026-10-09:

https://g.alicdn.com/sail-web/wan-static-resources/0.0.30/images/favicon.ico

The asset is bundled locally and used to identify the Wan model. It is not part of the Lobe Icons assets covered by `lobe-icons.txt`.
